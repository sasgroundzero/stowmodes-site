A simple, honest signal for phone-free classrooms.

Stow Modes shows a teacher, at a glance, whether each student has the Stow Modes app open during class. Phones go away, the lesson stays the focus, and nobody is watching what students do on their phones.

How it works

Three steps, no new hardware.

01

The teacher sets up the class

Upload the class roster and print a slip for each student. Each slip carries a single-use code.

02

Students link their phone once

A student enters the code from their slip in the Stow Modes app. After that, they open the app when class starts.

03

The teacher sees one simple view

Every student appears as a tile, in roster order, in the teacher's web console.

What the teacher sees

Four plain states.

FocusedThe app is open.
Broke focusThe app was left briefly.
AwayThe app is not open.
Not reportingNo signal right now.

Focused means the app is open. It does not mean a student is paying attention, and Stow Modes never says it does. When we cannot know, the tile shows gray rather than guessing.

What it is not

A signal, not monitoring.

  • It cannot see other apps, messages, photos or browsing.
  • It does not use location, the camera or the microphone.
  • It does not lock or restrict the phone.
  • It does not grade, score, rank or flag students.
  • It sends no alerts about individual students.
  • It does not sell or share student data for advertising.
